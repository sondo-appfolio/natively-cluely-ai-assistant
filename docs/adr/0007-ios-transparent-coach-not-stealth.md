# iOS uses transparent coach positioning, not stealth

The iOS product adopts **transparent-coach-positioning**: practice and live coaching with clear recording indicators, and no “undetectable,” “invisible overlay,” or proctoring-evasion claims. Desktop may keep stealth messaging; iOS must not claim overlay or screen-share invisibility. App Review risk (misleading features, recording indication, meeting-app ToS) makes stealth-parity marketing a deliberate non-goal (`standalone-ios-non-goals`).
